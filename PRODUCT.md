# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Current and prospective Rushden Nails clients who want to review services, compare prices, find the salon, and book quickly, often from a mobile phone.

## Product Purpose

Present Rushden Nails as a trusted local nail salon, communicate its treatments and prices clearly, and make contacting or booking the salon effortless.

## Brand Personality

Luxurious, gentle, and professional. The experience should feel welcoming, calm, polished, and trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid loud discount-salon styling, cluttered price lists, generic beauty templates, overly playful visuals, and interactions that distract from services or booking.

## Design Principles

- Make services and prices easy to scan.
- Preserve a refined, welcoming salon atmosphere.
- Keep booking and contact actions obvious.
- Let real nail imagery establish trust and quality.
- Prioritize comfortable mobile reading and navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear, readable text, sufficient contrast, keyboard-friendly navigation, reduced-motion support, meaningful image alternatives, and a mobile-first layout. No additional compliance target has been specified.
